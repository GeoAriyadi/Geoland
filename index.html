GeoLand Indonesia Platform

AI-Powered National Location Intelligence Platform

GeoLand Indonesia is an AI-powered Location Intelligence platform engineered to transform nationwide geospatial datasets into actionable spatial insights. The platform integrates more than 120 million building footprints, nationwide road networks, administrative boundaries, points of interest (POI), satellite data, and spatial indicators into a unified geospatial intelligence ecosystem designed for large-scale analysis across Indonesia.

Built on a scalable spatial data architecture, GeoLand processes complex geographic information through PostgreSQL/PostGIS, spatial indexing, hexagonal aggregation, and optimized vector tile delivery. The platform converts raw geospatial data into intelligent analytical layers, enabling users to explore regional characteristics, evaluate accessibility, discover strategic locations, analyze urban growth patterns, and support location-based decision making across multiple industries.

Beyond traditional mapping visualization, GeoLand Indonesia combines AI-assisted spatial analytics, building-level intelligence, property insights, POI-based commercial analysis, and interactive map visualization into a lightweight high-performance platform. Advanced optimization techniques such as spatial preprocessing, database indexing, and vector-based rendering ensure that nationwide datasets remain responsive and accessible through a modern web interface.

The platform demonstrates the integration of modern geospatial engineering technologies including PostgreSQL/PostGIS, MapLibre, vector tiles, 3D visualization, spatial data pipelines, and AI-driven recommendation systems. GeoLand Indonesia is designed as a scalable Location Intelligence solution for strategic planning, real estate analysis, infrastructure development, retail expansion, investment assessment, and regional decision support.
